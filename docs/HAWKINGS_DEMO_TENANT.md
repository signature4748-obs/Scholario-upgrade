## Academic structure (Phase 2 contract)

15 classes — Nursery, LKG, IKG, Classes 1–12 — with **exactly one section
(A) per class**. Canonical labels: `Nursery-A`, `LKG-A`, `IKG-A`, `1-A` …
`12-A`. Every class has a homeroom (`Room N0A … Room 120A`) + 5 special
rooms (Science Lab, Computer Lab, Library, Staff Room, Principal's Office).

Subjects (canonical, school-scoped): English, Hindi, Mathematics,
Environmental Studies, Science, Social Science, Sanskrit, Physics,
Chemistry, Biology, Computer Education + non-examinable Drawing and
Games & PE. The pre-primary and primary wings are taught by their class
teachers; classes 6–12 by the subject specialists
(`prisma/hawkings-corpus.ts` CSA_MATRIX — 86 assignments).

- **Exams** — PA1 (13–17 Jul, COMPLETED + Declared, max 50), Half-Yearly

- **Fees** — Term 1 + Term 2 tuition, annual charges, exam fee per

  months, skip-if-exists.
- **Timetable** — Mon–Sat × 8 periods (08:00–15:00), conflict-free
  (teacher loads capped at 37/48 slots).
- **Curriculum + lesson planning** — 6–12 chapters auto-instantiated from

- **Proctoring** — 219 papers across 3 exams, seat assignments, historical

- **Communication** — parent↔teacher threads, teacher↔principal threads,

  (published + public-facing); teacher-hub parent-connect corpus (5

- **Homework** — 9 CSA-teacher assignments across classes 1–12.
- **Library** — 12 authentic titles (Premchand, Kalam, NCERT, Panchatantra…).

## Determinism & pipeline

The corpus is generated by the PURE module `prisma/hawkings-corpus.ts`
(fixed PRNG seeds — identical output every run, every environment).
`bun run seed:demo` runs the 12-step pipeline; re-running resets the demo
tenant to canonical state (Green Valley stays honest-empty). The
production demo-tenant transform uses the same pipeline under the explicit
double opt-in (`SCHOLARIO_DEMO_TRANSFORM_CONSENT`, see
`prisma/seed-guard.ts`).
